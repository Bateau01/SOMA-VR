Body damping setter scales authored damping by 0.02 into body +0x1c0..+0x1cc.

Four direct references found at +0x22359, +0x229a8, +0x2f71b, +0x2f74b.
Its two nested helpers perform local vector scale/subtract; no script callbacks.
The experiment halves the decay exponent for that call, then restores all four
fields. It does not adjust gravity, forces, authored data or save contents.

Classification: grace periods, TTLs and polling cadence scale by two ticks.
Actual solver step identities, stale-contact rejection, ownership generations,
map maturity boundaries and collision iteration budgets retain real step counts.
Per-step drag bounds/deadzones scale down; physical force/velocity units do not.
Slider target velocity and door inverse-recurrence adapters already use dt.
OpenXR timestamps, frame histories and display pacing are not changed.

The Bunker evidence: MenuHandler's 120 setting selects native logic timer rate
through GameplayUpdateRate; no swap to a different Newton DLL is required.
The SOMA mod uses no copied Bunker binaries or scripts.

Tests use a private Newton world and the isolated SOMA validation copy.
The first tiny-body hinge fixture was unstable at the native 60 Hz baseline;
it was replaced with a larger door-sized body and matching inertia. The final
comparison is in newton-comparison.json. Numerical paths need not be identical.
An initial uncorrected damping comparison failed (5-second velocities about
0.406 vs 0.165); the corrected comparison passes.

Not proved here: complete story playthrough, headset hand/contact feel,
CPU headroom in complex maps, every joint/solver/contact configuration,
and unsupported executable/library revisions. Headset testing is deferred.
